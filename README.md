📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI
Project Overview

This project is an interactive Super Store Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, profit, customer segments, shipping methods, payment modes, and regional trends.
It also includes a 15-day sales forecasting dashboard to help businesses predict future sales and support data-driven decision-making.

📌 Business Questions Answered
1. Which product category generates the highest sales?
Answer: The Technology category generates the highest sales with approximately 0.64M, followed by Furniture (0.47M) and Office Supplies (0.45M).

2. Which customer segment contributes the most revenue?
Answer: The Consumer segment contributes the highest revenue, accounting for 48% of total sales. The Corporate segment contributes 33%, while the Home Office segment contributes 19%.

3. Which payment mode is used most frequently?
Answer: Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most frequently used payment mode, representing 43% of all transactions. This is followed by Online Payments (35%) and Cards (22%).

4. Which region and state perform best?
Answer: West is the best-performing region, contributing 33% of total sales.
California is the highest-performing state with approximately 0.34M in sales, followed by New York (0.19M) and Texas (0.12M).

5. How do monthly sales and profits change over time?
Answer: The dashboard includes monthly sales and profit trend charts for 2019–2020, allowing users to compare performance across months and identify seasonal patterns. The visualizations show that sales and profits fluctuate throughout the year, helping businesses monitor growth trends and peak sales periods.

6. What are the expected sales over the next 15 days?
Answer: The dashboard includes a 15-Day Sales Forecast that predicts upcoming sales based on historical trends. The forecast indicates expected daily sales values ranging from approximately 0.1K to 10.6K, helping businesses anticipate future demand and support inventory and operational planning.
